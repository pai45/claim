Fallback Control

Fallback Control enables Split Pay. When an eligible wallet has a low balance, its available balance is used first and only the remaining amount is taken from your Reimbursement Wallet.

  • Split Pay applies only to eligible Meal or Fuel merchant payments.
  • You will see the wallet split before confirming payment.
  • Payment is blocked when the combined available balance is insufficient.

    Split Pay is on for Meal Wallet. Its available balance will be used first and only the remaining amount will be deducted from Reimbursement Wallet.

  • Fuel Wallet

    Split Pay is on for Fuel Wallet. Its available balance will be used first and only the remaining amount will be deducted from Reimbursement Wallet.
